— about us —

A boutique mobile spa built for parents who want more than a party — a curated experience their child will remember.

The Pink Spa Bus opened in 2018 with a simple idea: bring the calm, considered feeling of a luxury spa to children's celebrations, in a setting parents can trust completely.

Our hostesses are trained for working with children, every product is selected for sensitive skin, and the equipment is cleaned and reset between every booking. Every detail — from the music playing when guests arrive to the keepsake they take home — is curated by hand.

Participation Disclosure

The Pink Spa Bus is a mobile spa providing age-appropriate beauty & wellness experiences for children. By signing this disclosure, the parent or legal guardian acknowledges:

(1) All products used are formulated for sensitive skin and are non-toxic. (2) Each child's known allergies have been disclosed. (3) Activities are supervised at all times by trained staff. (4) Photographs taken during the event are for private use only and not published without consent.

The parent or legal guardian assumes general responsibility for any pre-existing conditions not disclosed at booking, and authorises emergency care if reasonably required during the programme.
