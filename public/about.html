— about us —

A pink spa bus that pulls up to your door for glamour, fun, and amazing moments — a celebration your child will never forget.

We started The Pink Spa Bus with the goal of providing unique and personalized event experiences. The bus arrives fully styled — glam mirror stations, soft seating, music, and a hostess — so the guest of honour and her friends step into a world made just for them.

Every party is paced for children and bilingual for every family. We bring the products, the setup, and the photo moments; you bring the birthday girl. Based in Central Florida.

The Pink Spa Bus was born from the idea of bringing the magic of a spa directly to parties and special events. More than just a service, it is an accessible luxury experience where every detail is carefully designed to make young girls feel like true stars. The brand embodies joy, confidence, and celebration, creating special moments that will remain in the memories of the girls and their families.
