What it feels like
to be the guest of honour.

From the moment we arrive, the guest of honour and her friends step into a world arranged just for them — soft robes, a glam table set with their names, and a hostess who treats every detail like a couture appointment.

Every programme is paced for children — gentle, age-appropriate, and ends on a photo moment to keep.

The Pink Spa Bus is a unique experience on wheels, designed to transform any celebration into a magical, glamorous occasion. This innovative pink bus is not just a vehicle, but a world of sparkle, fun, and style, where girls can enjoy an unforgettable spa experience in a themed, fantasy-filled setting.
